- [x] **1. Studio shell** (2026-11-23): consent, a prompt card with Record, Stop, playback, Record again and Skip (keys R, N, S), the live input level and a level verdict, lossless WAV takes kept under `recordings/human/`, a Takes screen, and a JSONL manifest. The en-GB starter set (about ten minutes of speech) is seeded from `curriculum.py`. The capture, WAV and level code is shared: strata-react's `useRecorder`/`InputLevel`/`TakeLevel` and strata-python's `wav.read_wav`.
- [x] **2. Auto-crop and score** (2026-11-24): the crop from Whisper's word timings (through strata-models' `transcribe` role), clipping, level and noise floor (strata-python's `wav.measure`), the word error rate against the prompt (strata-python's `wer`), and the studio asking again at once for a take that fails, with Keep it anyway. A take the models service cannot hear yet is kept as `unchecked`, and `strata-voice score` judges it later. Importing existing audio waits: Jonathan has little yet and will gather it (2026-11-24).
- [x] **3. Prompt generation and coverage** (2026-11-24): a cloud model (Claude Sonnet by default, through strata-models' new `write` role) writes batches of prompts aimed at what is missing; a local ranker orders them by new coverage per second of the speaker's time, over RP phonemes and phoneme pairs (espeak-ng), word stress, length, sentence type and style, ranked again every 5 usable takes. The starter set stays first. A Coverage screen shows what is said and missing, with Write more prompts. Not yet: teacher disagreement and student error in the score (they need a model), and generated prompts held out for evaluation.
- [ ] **4. Pilot**: a session of about 8 to 10 minutes, then tune the thresholds from what it shows.
  - [x] The report (2026-11-24): `strata-voice pilot` reads the newest session (or `--since` a day) against each threshold in `scoring.py`: the spread measured, how many failed, how many nearly did, the wait before the first word and after the last, takes per prompt, and seconds of kept speech per minute at the microphone.
  - [x] The hands-free studio (2026-11-25), which makes the session a straight read: see 4a.
  - [ ] Jonathan records the session: start strata-models (so takes are heard), open the studio (or Hands-free), read the starter set from the top, and keep going for 8 to 10 minutes without changing the room or the microphone between takes.
  - [ ] Run `strata-voice score` (for any take recorded while strata-models was busy), then `strata-voice pilot --json`, and tune: a limit that fails often over a tight spread is wrong for this setup; many near misses say the room or the microphone is borderline. A changed limit is followed by `strata-voice score --all`.
- [x] **4a. Hands-free studio** (2026-11-25, Jonathan: "always listening ... i can just speak without clicking anything"): the Hands-free screen keeps the microphone open (strata-react's `useAutoRecorder` cuts each stretch of speech out of it), gives each take to the prompt showing when the speaker started it, and moves on at once; takes are kept and judged in the background, in the order said, and one judged `retry` puts its prompt back in the queue after the prompt being read. The prompts still to read are a paged list (`GET /api/queue`). Scoring now finds the prompt's words inside a take (strata-python's `wer.find`), so a false start or a word to someone else is cropped out rather than failing it. One transcription pass for now (Whisper turbo, well under a second a take); a quicker first pass (`transcribe-fast`) only if the pilot shows the verdicts lagging (Jonathan's choice, 2026-11-25).
- [x] **4b. Upload** (2026-11-26, Jonathan: "i found a recording of me ... take an audio file of any format and then process - normalize, transcribe, extract useful bits, ingest"): the Upload screen and `strata-voice ingest FILE` take any file ffmpeg reads. Each one is a job on strata-python's queue (`ingest.py`), in these stages:
  - level it: one gain for the whole file to a -1 dBFS peak, at most +30 dB, as a 24-bit mono WAV at the source rate (capped at 48 kHz);
  - transcribe it with the `transcribe` role;
  - cut it into sentences at sentence ends, at gaps over 0.8 s, and at the longest pause of anything over 15 s, keeping 1 s and two words or more;
  - check each sentence's voice with strata-models' new `speaker-embedding` role (ECAPA). The comparison is against the mean of the speaker's good studio takes, or, with none yet, the voice most of the recording is in;
  - judge each sentence like a studio take (`scoring.score`). A sentence whose voice matches by under 0.4 is `retry` with a `speaker` problem, and Keep anyway on the Takes screen overrides it.
  Sentences are prompts of kind `found` (source `upload`), kept out of the studio queue and counted in coverage. The manifest marks them `found`, with their file and place.
  - [ ] On the deploy machine: `scripts/setup_worker_venv.sh speaker` in strata-models and its GPU test, then bring in one real recording and look at the similarity spread. The 0.4 is a first guess, to tune like step 4's thresholds.
